Overview

Banele Driving is a professional driving school serving learners across Durban and KwaZulu-Natal. We believe learning to drive should be safe, patient, and confidence-building — not rushed or stressful.

Our instructors are patient, qualified, and experienced with nervous first-timers. Whether you're booking your first lesson or preparing for the K53 test, we'll get you road-ready.
