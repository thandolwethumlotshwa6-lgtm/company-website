Mlotshwa Trading Corporations ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

This policy applies to all six of our businesses: Family Trust, Shoe Wash, Ndabezitha Transportation, Precious Rentals, Banele Driving, and Mlotshwa Productions.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information, depending on which of our services you use:

  • Identity information: name, surname, ID number, date of birth.
  • Contact information: phone number, email address, physical address.
  • Financial information: banking details (only when required — e.g. for Family Trust loan applications or Precious Rentals leases).
  • Business information: company name, registration details (for commercial clients).
  • Service information: details about the service you're enquiring about or booking.
  • Website data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (see section 7).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for the purposes for which it was provided, including:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To deliver the services you've requested (e.g. cleaning your shoes, driving lessons, transport bookings).
  • To process applications (e.g. loans through Family Trust, rental applications through Precious Rentals).
  • To communicate with you about your bookings, orders, or accounts.
  • To improve our services and website experience.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted partners who help us run our business (e.g. payment processors, delivery partners), bound by confidentiality.
  • Legal requirements: where we're required by law, court order, or a regulator to disclose information.
  • Business transfers: in the unlikely event of a merger or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent: where you've explicitly agreed to sharing.

4. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected — or as required by law.

  • Enquiries: up to 12 months after last contact.
  • Service records: as long as the service is active, plus a reasonable archive period.
  • Financial records: as required by South African tax and financial laws (typically 5 years).

When no longer needed, your information is securely deleted or anonymised.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, damage, and unauthorised access. These include:

  • Restricted access — only staff who need the information can access it.
  • Secure storage of physical and digital records.
  • Regular review of our practices and systems.
  • Confidentiality agreements with employees and service providers.

While no system is 100% secure, we're committed to protecting your information to the best of our ability and to notifying you promptly if a breach occurs.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your information (where legally possible).
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10.

7. Cookies & Website Data

Our website may use basic cookies and similar technologies to help it function properly and to understand how visitors use it. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking you across other websites.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may then not work as intended.

8. Children's Privacy

Some of our services (e.g. Banele Driving lessons) may involve minors. We collect children's information only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, and always in accordance with POPIA.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, if the changes are significant, notify you through our website or by email.
