Overview

Family Trust is a community-focused financial services company providing fast, fair loans to families and small businesses across Durban and KwaZulu-Natal. We believe access to honest, affordable credit shouldn't be complicated — so we've made it simple.

From school fees to stock purchases, emergency repairs to business expansion, Family Trust helps people move forward when they need it most. No endless paperwork. No hidden fees. Just support, when it matters.
